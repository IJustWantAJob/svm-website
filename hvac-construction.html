Overview

Built for any project, any scale

At the core of every building and facility you'll find complex HVAC and Plumbing Systems. With our office and fabrication shop expanding to over 220,000 SF, over 4 buildings on 15+ acres, SVM is ready for any project.

From inception to close-out, our Project Managers are integral to the design process, working side-by-side with the General Contractor, Architect, and building owner to ensure a seamless and successful outcome for each project.

BIM & Prefabrication

Precision through technology

SVM leverages advanced Building Information Modeling (BIM) and 3D coordination to identify and resolve conflicts before they reach the field. Our dedicated BIM team works directly with project stakeholders to ensure every component is accounted for in a fully coordinated digital model.

Our in-house fabrication capabilities allow us to prefabricate ductwork, piping assemblies, and mechanical systems offsite, reducing installation time, minimizing waste, and improving quality control. This approach delivers faster project timelines and a cleaner, safer job site.

Energy Management

Efficiency by design

SVM designs and installs high-performance HVAC systems with energy efficiency at the forefront. From variable refrigerant flow (VRF) systems to advanced building automation and controls, our team ensures that every system is optimized for both comfort and energy savings.

We work closely with building owners and engineers to evaluate lifecycle costs, identify energy rebate opportunities, and implement solutions that reduce operating expenses while meeting California's stringent Title 24 energy codes and sustainability goals.
