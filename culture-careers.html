Culture & Careers

At SVM, we believe great people build great projects. Our culture is rooted in doing the right thing, staying curious, working collaboratively, and living balanced.

Life at SVM

Where great people build great things

At SVM, we believe great people build great projects. Our culture is rooted in values that guide daily team support. We emphasize employee empowerment through education, mentorship, and hands-on learning.

Programs include leadership training, team-building events, wellness initiatives, and community service. Whether you are starting your career or bringing decades of experience, we value your contributions and shared success.

Community Involvement

Giving back

Since 2018, SVM has partnered with Rebuilding Together Silicon Valley for home repairs throughout Santa Clara County. Our employees volunteer their skills and time as part of the core values that drive everything we do.

We also host annual Stanford Blood Center blood drives for employee and community participation. Giving back is not an afterthought at SVM, it is part of who we are.

Work Hard, Play Hard

Balance built in

We maintain an on-site gym with fitness classes including bootcamp, yoga, and Pilates to support work-life balance. Our team stays active together through golf club, softball leagues, basketball, mountain biking, and hiking groups.

Core Values

What drives us

Do the right thing

Integrity in every interaction. We stand behind our work and our word. No shortcuts, no excuses.

Be curious

From VRF training for apprentices to new certifications for veterans, we never stop learning. Technology moves fast and so do we.

Work collaboratively

Three offices, one standard. Our technicians, PMs, and engineers share knowledge across every project and every region.

Live balanced

On-site gyms, team sports leagues, community volunteering. We build systems that sustain, and that includes taking care of our people.
